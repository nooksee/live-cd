# Contributors and project stewardship

LiveUSB Creator carries forward the GPL-licensed lineage of Customizer and
LiveCD Creator while beginning a new product lifecycle under its current
name, implementation, and direction. This file records current stewardship,
AI collaboration identities, lifecycle roles, and attribution practice
without displacing historical credit.

## Current product ownership

  maintainer, source of product intent, and final acceptance authority.

Historical repository and copyright notices identify Kevin Atwood for work
performed during the earlier LiveCD Creator period. Those notices remain
unchanged as provenance. Current project and documentation credit uses
Kevin Thomas.

## Product lifecycle roles

1. Kevin Thomas defines product intent, priorities, and final acceptance.
2. George Prime converts that intent into bounded phases, assignments,
   integration decisions, and release gates.
3. Claude Devens reviews product behavior, architecture, safety, and
   unresolved risk independently from the primary implementation lane.
4. Jacob Codex owns bounded implementation and verification lanes assigned
   by George Prime.
5. Material changes receive human acceptance before they become a product
   release.

One active writer owns each code or documentation surface during coordinated
work. Review, testing, and analysis may proceed concurrently when their
surfaces do not overlap.

## Attribution and licensing practice

- Repository content remains under the GNU General Public License version 2
  or, at the recipient's option, any later version, except where an individual
  file carries a different compatible notice.
- Historical copyright, authorship, and upstream notices remain attached to
  the work they describe.
- Project policy does not require per-line AI watermarks. Material AI
  collaboration is disclosed here and may also be recorded in commit,
  handoff, review, and release evidence.
- AI-assisted work is not represented as solely human-generated.
- Every contribution remains subject to human review, license compatibility,
  provenance review, and the same technical acceptance standards regardless
  of whether a human or AI collaborator proposed it.
